▶ Common questions

What's the single best local model right now?

There isn't one — “best” depends on your VRAM and mission. A 7B that runs fast on your card beats a 70B that won't load. Use the targeting tool above for a pick matched to your exact setup.

Bigger model or higher quantization?

The site's working rule: a smaller model at a higher quantization usually beats a larger model at a lower one, especially for coding and structured output. The exception is broad general knowledge, where sheer parameter count carries information no precision level recovers. Either way, avoid dropping below 4-bit; that is where quality falls off fastest. The quantization guide has the tradeoff table.

Can I run a model the tool says won't fit?

Sometimes — with a smaller quant, shorter context, or CPU offload (much slower). The “won't fit” verdict assumes a usable configuration; treat it as the safe default, not an absolute wall.

DEBRIEF Using the Recommender

The Recommender turns two facts about your situation into a short list of models: what you want the model to do, and how much VRAM you have to do it in. It runs in three phases and ends with named models you can pull today. Nothing in the ranking is paid for.

How the three steps work

Phase 01 is the mission. Choosing chat, coding, reasoning, embeddings, or vision decides which models are eligible at all, because an embedding model and a chat model are not interchangeable however the VRAM works out. Phase 02 is hardware. Pick a preset tier, select a specific GPU from the dropdown, or type a custom VRAM figure; this sets the ceiling on how large a model can be. Phase 03 returns the ranked picks with a fit verdict on each. From there you can compare the top two side by side, or hand the result to the Stack Builder to see how it sits alongside anything else you plan to keep loaded.

What the recommendations rest on

The picks come out of the site catalog, whose entries are written and maintained by hand: VRAM requirement at a stated quantization, context window, license, and the use-case tags that make a model eligible for a given mission. Fit uses the same arithmetic as the Stack Builder, weights being roughly parameters times 0.5 bytes at 4-bit plus headroom for the KV cache. There is no scraped leaderboard behind the order, and no user ratings anywhere on this site.

What it deliberately does not do

It does not carry affiliate links. No link here earns a commission, and there is no sponsored listing, paid placement, or purchased ranking: a model's presence and its position are editorial decisions, as set out on the about page. It also does not inspect your machine. You tell it what you have, nothing is uploaded, and no account is involved. And it will not name a single best local model, because the best model is the one that fits your card and your task.

Where to go next

If the mission is coding, the best local coding LLM guide goes deeper than a ranked list can, comparing the code-specialized families by what they are actually good at. If you would rather browse than be told, the full catalog is the place to do it, with the same VRAM and license detail on every entry. If you are still choosing hardware, the PC Builder plans a machine around the model you want to run.
