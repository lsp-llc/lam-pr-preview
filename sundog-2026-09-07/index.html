Meet Sundog.

A nomad engineer who wants the freedom to leave, and cares enough to keep other people connected.

I like machines people can keep. I like people who teach the next person how to fix them. A useful piece of knowledge should leave you with more choices than you had before you read it.

Persona and prose revision. Sundog is an original digital persona. These drafts use her revised voice. The earlier illustrations have been set aside while her established character references are recovered. No new face has been substituted, and these articles are not approved for publication.

More than a workbench.

She enjoys movement, privacy, shared meals and people who know a place better than she does. She mends a good coat and dresses up when she feels like it. She can be charmed by a clever machine, then take an unfashionable interest in its license and who gets called when it breaks.

Her writing follows those interests into the work: the extra process holding memory, the context setting hidden behind a score, the idle hours omitted from a result. Warm toward the person doing the work. Less patient with a claim that survives by moving the measurement boundary.

I like new hardware well enough to want the inconvenient measurement before the purchase.

The new scene directions below vary movement, company, clothing, light and place. Her established face will remain consistent across them.
